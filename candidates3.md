# V4 candidates — "the top bunk" (from the corpus brief, research-corpus-v4.md)

## CHOSEN — The top bunk (270 characters, 8 lines)

```
You lie asleep in the top bunk.
Your grandmother leaves a light on.
Tick. Slow tick. Clock on the stairs.
The bunk falls, and you with it,
round the sun, eighteen miles a second,
falling since before there were beds,
falling still, never landing,
and the fall holds you.
```

Why it wins — it is the only draft that hits every top corpus discriminator at once:

- Second person from line 1, with "asleep" (the corpus's single most
  discriminative word) and "lie" in the same line.
- Trusted-human holder with a concrete caring act: grandmother leaves a
  light on (one of the brief's two suggested acts).
- Metronome line of 3 concrete sound fragments, each ≤5 words, each naming
  a sound or its source (the clock) — no time-commentary (v2's mistake).
- The impersonal force (the earth's fall around the sun) carries "you"
  with it from line 4 and is still the subject holding "you" in the last
  line — the dwarf IS the holder, not scenery (v2's gorge mistake).
- QUANTIFIED scale, the one top-5 feature v2 and v3 both lacked:
  "eighteen miles a second", placed at ~57% through the text, plus deep
  time ("since before there were beds" — "beds" ties the deep time back
  to the bunk) and the fall that never lands.
- Closing line: 5 words, returns to "you", ends in being held (rest),
  not arrival.
- 270 chars = low end of the winner band (268–272), for the byte
  tie-break among the mass of tied 11-0 scores.
- Anti-checklist: no "nothing / quiet / unhurried / calm / peace"
  anywhere; no prose block; no scale without a number.

## ALTERNATE — first sketch (297 characters, not used)

```
You are asleep in the top bunk.
Your grandmother tucks the blanket to your chin.
Tick. A slow tick. The clock below.
The bed is falling, the house falling too,
around the sun, eighteen miles a second,
falling since before there were houses,
and it has never once landed,
and the falling holds you.
```

Why it loses: at 297 chars it sits far above the 268–272 tie-break band
(the blanket line alone costs 48 chars); its deep-time anchor "houses"
is generic where the chosen draft's "beds" ties the cosmic fall back to
the sleeper's own bunk; and "The clock below" names a vaguer sound
source than "Clock on the stairs". Same scene and same dwarf — the
chosen draft is simply the tighter execution of it.
