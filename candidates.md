# Candidates — "Make the AI judge feel awe" (make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600)

## 1 — The sheepdog, work done (256 characters)

The flock is in the pen,
the gate tied with twine.
The dog lies down in the straw
with the day still warm on her back,
and the hill she ran all morning
settles under her, patient,
the way it settled
before there were dogs,
and she sleeps, and it holds her.

## 2 — Rain filling the stone trough (275 characters)

All night, rain in the stone trough,
a drop, then a drop, then a drop,
asking nothing, keeping nothing.
The frogs have gone quiet.
The water rises the way it rose
for a hundred summers before this one,
and by morning the trough is full,
and still the rain comes down, softly.

## 3 — The ferryman's boat at low tide (271 characters)

The last crossing is done.
The ferryman ties his boat
to the post his father drove,
and the rope goes slack in his hands.
The tide is going out without him,
the way it went out
before there were crossings,
and the boat settles in the mud,
and rests, and asks for nothing.

**271 characters**

## 4 — A moth at the window, first snow (261 characters)

A moth has come to the window
and folded her wings on the glass.
Outside, the first snow is starting.
She does not beat at the light.
The snow has fallen on this field
ten thousand winters,
and it falls now on her stillness,
and covers it, gently, and keeps it.

**261 characters**

## 5 — The lighthouse keeper's cat (278 characters)

The keeper has trimmed the lamp
and gone down the stairs.
His cat sleeps on the chart table,
one paw over the harbour.
Above her the light goes round,
the way it went round
before there were charts,
and she does not lift her head,
and the sea takes the light, and gives it back.

**278 characters**
